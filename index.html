Welcome to the tower

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries.

  • Each guess must be a 5-letter word. Press Enter to submit.
  • After each guess, the tiles change color to show how close you are.

Letters can repeat. A letter is only colored as many times as it appears in the word.

Find the word and a new tab opens where you can enter the prize draw. You can also share your result as colored squares.
